| Source tags | Every content row has one of three tags. `[實證]`: a source a person can check, or an `[AI整理]` summary that a person checked. `[AI整理]`: AI summarized it from a real source, and no person has checked it yet. The source is the host's citizen data (the 「市長，你給我聽好了」 data, 10 or more messages, with counts) or a news report (the cell says 「新聞報導指出…」, has a 【待補：查證…】 item, and the appendix names the outlet and gives the link). `[AI模擬]`: AI made it up as a likely view; no data behind it. | Needs-based format | Wants (我想要…) are raw ideas of a solution; needs (以便…) are the stakeholder's own benefit. A need never goes beyond the stakeholder, so needs never conflict. Shared needs get groups: 相同需求編號 `N01`, `N02`… in a step 4 column after 以便…, and a `### 相同需求群組` table. Each group has 2 or more stories from 2 or more different stakeholders (ETBlue, 2026-10-20): one stakeholder's need in several stories is still one need. The step 2 audit rewrites a want in 以便… or a need in 我想要…, and a 以便… about other people. An organization's need is written as its own mission: 「做到某件事，達成這個組織被交付或承擔的使命」 (ETBlue, 2026-10-20). A rewrite of 題目原文 or citizen data becomes `[AI整理]`; a rewrite of `[AI模擬]` stays `[AI模擬]`. Every rewrite is listed (old → new) in step 3 (#2: 3.9). `analysis_common.needs_format` finds the format by the `### 相同需求群組` heading. #1, #2 and #8 use this format.
